AfterGram notifications

Use the ntfy iPhone app for notifications. Your Telegram message stays on your server; ntfy.sh only relays a generic wake-up ping.

The setup page stores the server and owner token in this browser on this device only. After setup, new alerts come from ntfy; tapping an alert attempts to open the matching AfterGram chat.
